# agntux-jira

Brings your Jira issues into AgntUX so the work that needs you shows up in one place. See assignments, replies on watched issues, and work that's stalling — then comment, transition, assign, edit, or log time directly from AgntUX.

## What you can do

- **See your work.** Watch your Jira projects for new issues, replies on issues you're watching, and assignments to you.
- **Comment on issues.** Reply directly from AgntUX with full formatting.
- **Change status.** Transition issues between workflow states without leaving AgntUX.
- **Assign work.** Move issues between team members or self-assign.
- **Edit details.** Update priority, labels, summary, or other fields.
- **Log time.** Record work hours on an issue.

## How it works

The plugin syncs your visible Jira projects every 60 minutes during your working hours (7am–7pm, weekdays only in your local timezone). It flags issues that need your attention:

- Issues assigned to you
- Open replies or @-mentions on issues you're watching
- Assignments approaching their SLA

When you spot something that needs action, use one of five UI handlers to comment, transition, assign, edit, or log time — all without switching apps.

## Requirements

- Active Jira Cloud account
- AgntUX host with the `agntux-core` plugin installed
- Access to the Atlassian Connector via your host's MCP settings
