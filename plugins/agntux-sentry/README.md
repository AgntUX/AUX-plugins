# agntux-sentry

**Bring your Sentry issues into AgntUX and resolve them without leaving your workflow.**

This plugin checks your Sentry projects every 30 minutes during weekday business hours (7am–7pm, local time) and surfaces the errors and problems that need you — new issues, escalating issues, and issues waiting for assignment.

When you spot an issue that needs action, use these three tools to respond without switching apps:

- **Resolve** — Mark an issue as resolved.
- **Ignore/Archive** — Archive or ignore an issue.
- **Assign** — Assign the issue to a team member or team.

## Requirements

- Active Sentry account
- AgntUX with `agntux-core` installed
- Sentry connected to AgntUX
