# agntux-granola

See your Granola meeting notes in AgntUX—transcripts, summaries, action items, and follow-ups in one place.

## What it does

The agntux-granola plugin brings your Granola AI meeting notes into your AgntUX knowledge store. Every sync pass checks for new meetings and surfaces action items, decisions, and key takeaways so they don't get lost between calls. When you spot something that needs attention, you can jump back to Granola to reply or take action.

### Who should use it

- Teams that rely on Granola for meeting AI summaries and transcripts
- Anyone tracking action items and decisions across multiple calls
- People who need meeting context available alongside other work signals

## How to install

1. Install the Granola connector in your AgntUX host.
2. Search for "agntux-granola" in the marketplace and add it to your account.
3. Type `/agntux-granola sync` or `/agntux-granola` to pull in your latest meetings.

## What it ingests

- **Meeting transcripts and summaries** — the full AI-generated notes and key takeaways
- **Attendees** — who was on the call
- **Action items and follow-ups** — what you or others need to do
- **Meeting metadata** — title, date, duration, and folder organization

## Commands

- `/agntux-granola` or `/agntux-granola sync` — Check for new meetings now
- `/agntux-granola {your question}` — Ask a question about your meetings (read-only lookup)
