# agntux-hubspot

**Bring your HubSpot deals, tasks, and customer records into AgntUX and manage them without leaving your workflow.**

This plugin checks your HubSpot account every 2 hours during weekday business hours (7am–7pm, local time) and surfaces the work that needs you — deals approaching close dates, tasks coming due or overdue, tickets waiting for replies, and recent customer activity.

When you spot a record that needs action, use these four tools to respond without switching apps:

- **Move deal to a new stage** — Transition between your sales pipeline stages.
- **Complete or reschedule task** — Mark tasks done or push the due date.
- **Log note or activity** — Add a note, call, email, or meeting to a record.
- **Reassign to another owner** — Transfer deals, tickets, or contacts to a team member.

## Requirements

- Active HubSpot account
- AgntUX with `agntux-core` installed
- HubSpot connected to AgntUX
